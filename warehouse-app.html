About

This app works alongside the Warehouse Manager Apps Script in Google Sheets.
The Apps Script pulls live data from Shopify and ShipStation into your Sheet.
This app reads from that Sheet to show your bin dashboard, pick list, and cycle counts.

Data is stored locally in your browser. Export CSV from Cycle Count to keep records.
